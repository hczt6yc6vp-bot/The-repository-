# Spoke & Shine — Bicycle Cleaning & Repair Website

A simple one-page website (black background, white text) with an online
appointment form that sends booking requests straight to your email.

## Files

- `index.html` — the page content
- `styles.css` — the black & white design
- `script.js` — mobile menu and the booking form

## Connect your email (2 minutes)

The booking form uses [FormSubmit](https://formsubmit.co), a free service
that forwards form submissions to your inbox. No account or server needed.

1. Open `index.html` and find `YOUR-EMAIL@example.com` (it appears in the
   booking form and in the Contact section). Replace it with your email.
2. Publish the site (see below).
3. Submit a test booking on your live site. FormSubmit will send an
   **activation email** to your address — click the link in it.
4. Done. Every booking request now arrives in your inbox, with the client's
   name, email, phone, service, date, time and message. Hit "Reply" to
   confirm with the client.

## Make it your own

Edit `index.html` to change the business name, services, prices, address,
hours and phone number.

## Publish for free with GitHub Pages

1. In this repository on GitHub, go to **Settings → Pages**.
2. Under "Branch", choose `main` and `/ (root)`, then click **Save**.
3. After a minute your site is live at
   `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repository-name>/`.
