# Olafsgarage.ch — Bicycle Cleaning & Repair Website

A simple one-page website (black background, white text) with an online
appointment form that sends booking requests straight to your email.

## Files

- `index.html` — the page content
- `styles.css` — the black & white design
- `script.js` — mobile menu and the booking form

## Email

Booking requests are sent to **olaf@eulers.ch** via
[FormSubmit](https://formsubmit.co) (free, no account needed).

After the site is online, submit one test booking. FormSubmit will send an
**activation email** to olaf@eulers.ch — click the link in it once. After
that, every booking arrives in that inbox with the client's details.

## Publish for free with GitHub Pages

1. In this repository on GitHub, go to **Settings → Pages**.
2. Under "Branch", choose `main` and `/ (root)`, then click **Save**.
3. After a minute your site is live at
   `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repository-name>/`.
